## Dataset facts (from validation, 2026-10-29)

- Naming: `test/test_00000.png` ... `test_03960.png` (index = row in `test_index.csv`); `train/` and `val/` keep the original CIHP ids (`0000006.png` ...), masks share the stem.
- Images RGB 320 x 320; masks 8-bit single channel (`L`), values 0-19 plus 255 for padding. 255 covers 28.6% of all mask pixels; per mask the valid share ranges 0.28-1.00 (val 0.42-1.00). No mask is background-only.
- 137 pairs of byte-identical images inside train (different ids, same bytes); only 6 pairs share an identical mask. On jointly valid pixels the two masks agree 97.8% at the median (min 56%), so these are mostly re-annotations with boundary or part-label differences, a small label-noise source. No duplicates across splits, so val/test are not leaked.
- Class share of valid pixels: background 59.7%, upper-clothes 9.9%, coat 7.6%, hair 4.8%, face 4.6%, pants 4.0%; rarest are sunglasses 0.04%, glove 0.06%, socks 0.12%, scarf 0.20%, shoes about 0.21% each, legs about 0.36% each. Left/right pairs (14/15, 16/17, 18/19) occur in nearly equal counts, which the label-aware flip in the template relies on.
- Train RGB mean [0.487, 0.448, 0.417], std [0.257, 0.250, 0.252], padding included; close to the ImageNet constants the template normalizes with.
